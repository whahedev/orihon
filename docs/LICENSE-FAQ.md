Short answers for the commercial model. The binding non-commercial text is [LICENSE](../LICENSE) (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0). Paid plans are described in [Pricing](PRICING.md). Paid Commercial Production is governed by the [Commercial License Agreement](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) (v1.0) when incorporated into an Order.

## Do I need a paid license to try Orihon?

No. Community covers personal use, education, research, non-commercial OSS, evaluation, proofs of concept and development **before** commercial launch. Install from npm and build the prototype.

You need a commercial license before **production commercial deployment**.

## What counts as commercial use?

Typical triggers: paid products, SaaS, revenue-generating sites/apps, client deliverables billed as commercial work, and internal tools that are part of a for-profit organization’s production stack when PolyForm Noncommercial does not cover that use.

When unsure, assume commercial production needs a paid plan and ask.

## Own product vs client work?

| Situation | License |
| --- | --- |
| You ship **your own** product, SaaS or internal app | **Indie / Startup / Business** by your Annual Revenue (or Enterprise) |
| You build or maintain software **for a client / third party** | **Agency** — required |

Indie / Startup / Business never cover third-party deliverables. A studio with $80k revenue still needs **Agency ($799)** for client projects; Indie ($149) is only for that studio’s **own** products.

## One package for everyone?

Yes. There is a single `orihon` npm package. Plans do not unlock ObjectManager, WebGL, MVT or other features — those ship to everyone. Plans change **rights and support**, not the tarball.

## Why no per-seat pricing?

Orihon licenses the **legal entity**, not each person who opens the repo. Designers, contractors, QA and backend engineers who touch map config do not each need a seat. Unlimited developers are included on every paid plan.

## Why no map-view limits?

Orihon runs in the browser against tile/providers you choose. Extra views do not create cost on our side. Paid plans include **unlimited map views**.

## How is Annual Revenue measured?

**Annual Revenue** means the Legal Entity’s gross revenue or equivalent turnover, excluding VAT / sales taxes, for the most recently completed fiscal year. Without a completed fiscal year, use a reasonable annualized figure from the latest available period.

- Indie ≤ $100k  
- Startup ≤ $1M  
- Business ≤ $20M  
- Above that → Enterprise  

Eligibility is checked at purchase and at each renewal. Crossing a band mid-Term is not a breach; move to the matching Plan at the next renewal.

**Agency** is not chosen by revenue. If the work is for a third party, Agency is required.

## Agency projects

**Agency ($799/year)** is required for Client Work and covers **Client Work only**.

It does **not** authorize your own First-Party Use. If you also ship your own Orihon-powered products, buy the matching Indie / Startup / Business / Enterprise Plan separately.

Coverage lasts while you are **contractually responsible** for development or ongoing maintenance — including when the app is hosted on the client’s infrastructure.

The client needs their own first-party license when they assume **independent** development/maintenance of Orihon functionality, or when your maintenance engagement ends. Inform the client in writing before that handoff.

## Freelancer built a site for a client — who pays?

- You develop or maintain the project for the client → **Agency** (not Indie/Startup, regardless of your revenue).
- Client independently owns and maintains Orihon functionality as their product → **client’s** Indie / Startup / Business / Enterprise license.

## What if I do not renew?

You keep **perpetual production rights** for applications that already shipped with an Orihon version obtained during the paid Term — existing production does not have to shut down.

Until you renew, you may not start new applications, upgrade to versions released after the Term, or receive support. Renewal is how you keep buying new development, new projects and support.

## SaaS allowed?

- **Your own SaaS** → Indie / Startup / Business / Enterprise (by Annual Revenue).
- **SaaS or sites for clients** → Agency while you develop/maintain under contractual responsibility; client first-party license after independent handoff.
- Community does not cover commercial SaaS production.

## Are there runtime license checks?

No. No license key API, no “license not found” console banner in production. Compliance is legal and commercial.

## Can I redistribute Orihon inside my product?

Follow PolyForm Noncommercial for Community use, and the Commercial License Agreement for paid use. Standalone OEM / white-label redistribution needs a separate addendum — ask rather than assuming.

## Founder pricing?

Founder pricing applies to the first 100 customers recorded as purchasing an eligible paid commercial license. The annual price of the originally purchased Plan is locked for the initial Term and the next three consecutive renewal Terms. Changing Plans uses the then-current price of the new Plan. Unless the Order states otherwise, a lapse in renewal ends the Founder price lock.

## Refunds and renewals

Annual Terms renew yearly for continued updates, new-application rights and support. Refund and termination details are set in the Order / Commercial License Agreement.
